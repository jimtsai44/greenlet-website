# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build tooling or framework (existing codebase).

## Users

Three audiences in New Zealand:
1. Cat owners who want a convenient, mess-contained way to grow fresh cat grass at home instead of buying loose trays or making repeat pet-store trips.
2. Parents of young children looking for a hands-on educational activity — planting, watching it sprout, then "styling" the grown grass like hair with safe scissors.
3. Households with little gardening experience who want easy, low-maintenance greenery for small spaces (desks, windowsills, apartments).

## Product Purpose

A small-batch, handmade product line (ceramic/terracotta cat-shaped planter dolls that grow cat grass as "hair," trimmable into hairstyles) sold direct-to-consumer online. Serves as a small-scale entrepreneurial market test for a New Zealand client. This homepage is the first deliverable; online checkout/payment is a later phase — current cart and newsletter interactions are front-end placeholders only.

## Positioning

Combines three usually-separate things into one object: fresh pet grass, a kids' grow-and-style creative activity, and a no-skill-required houseplant — in a doll-shaped ceramic planter, not an ordinary grass tray or nursery pot.

## Operating Context

- Sold and shipped within New Zealand (domestic shipping only).
- New Zealand biosecurity rules (MPI) govern seed and growing-media sale/import — the product uses biosecurity-compliant, certified seed and growing medium; this is presented as a trust signal, not just a compliance note.
- Site is a small static multi-page marketing site (`index.html`, `faq.html`, `subscribe.html`); no backend, payment processor, or live inventory system yet. Client plans to keep publishing articles going forward, so the header nav is the site's directory — FAQ and the newsletter signup were deliberately pulled off the homepage onto their own pages, reached only via nav click, to establish the pattern a future articles/journal section will extend.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product lineup (placeholder pricing in NZD pending client confirmation): classic doll planter, parent-child DIY styling twin-pack, seed/media refill pack, artisan limited ceramic edition.
- Cart is client-side only (localStorage), no real checkout — "Proceed to checkout" clearly communicates payment is coming soon.
- Newsletter signup is front-end only (no email backend wired up yet).
- Exact supplier, fulfillment, and shipping-zone details within NZ are undecided — currently using realistic-but-placeholder figures.

## Brand Commitments

Brand name: **Greenlet** (renamed from "KiKis Green" — the earlier name referenced a copyrighted animated character and was flagged as a trademark risk; "greenlet" is also a real word, a genus of small olive-green songbirds, which the client chose deliberately to anchor the new bird mascot). English-first copy and voice, written for a New Zealand audience. Recurring mascot: an original small green bird illustration, shown interacting with the black cat mascot. Visual system: same accent palette as the attic-bedroom direction (rose/sage/wood/gold), background simplified to white with deliberate negative space — decoration only where it carries meaning (the window scene, the mascots), not spread across the page.

## Evidence on Hand

No client-supplied logo, product photography, or testimonials yet. Original illustrated SVG artwork (doll planter, product icons) is used as a placeholder visual system and should be swapped for real photography once available.

## Product Principles

1. Make fresh cat grass genuinely convenient and mess-contained for cat owners.
2. Turn "growing something" into a repeatable, hands-on activity a parent and child do together — plant, sprout, trim/style.
3. Stay low-maintenance enough that a first-time grower succeeds without gardening experience.
4. Small-batch, handmade positioning supports premium pricing and a market-testing brand story.
5. NZ biosecurity compliance (certified seed, approved growing medium) is communicated as a feature that builds trust, not just a legal constraint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility expected (keyboard navigation, alt text, sufficient color contrast, visible focus states). No additional product-specific requirement has been established.
