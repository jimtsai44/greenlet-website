about us

It started with a wrecked balcony pot

Every time the cat went out for a chew, she came back having flattened half the grass. We figured if she was going to sit in it anyway, it might as well look like her. The first doll was hand-thrown on a wheel in a garage; now Greenlet makes them in small batches for cats and kids around New Zealand.
