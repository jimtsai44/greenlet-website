Growing Up the Wall

The real timeline behind every Greenlet doll — from a handful of seed to the first haircut, marked up like a kid's height chart on the doorframe.

The four stages

  1. Day 0

    Plant it

    Sprinkle the seed over the coir growing medium in the doll's head, water it once, and set it somewhere bright.

  2. Day 3–5

    The first pencil mark: it sprouts

    The first blades push up through the doll's head. This is the bit kids check on every morning.

  3. Day 10–14

    First trim

    Grass hits 8–10cm. Grab the mini scissors that came in the box and give it its first haircut.

  4. Every 7–10 days

    Keep marking the wall

    Water, trim, repeat. One seed packet keeps a doll growing for about 4–6 weeks before it's time to reseed.
